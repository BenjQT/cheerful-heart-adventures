Cheerful Heart Adventures

Join Ali, Wynea and Matthew on 12 math adventures that get kindergarteners ready for Grade 1. Tap a friend to say hi, then pick a game!

Pick an adventure

For grown-ups: Each game takes about 3 to 5 minutes and practices one skill: counting, shapes, patterns, comparing, adding and taking away, measuring, sorting, time, money, position words and number sense. The last game, the Friendship Race, reviews them all. Turn the sound on so the friends can read every instruction aloud. Stars are saved on this device.
