For grown-ups: Each game takes about 3 to 5 minutes and practices one skill: counting, shapes, patterns, comparing, adding and taking away, measuring, sorting, time, money, position words and number sense. The last game, the Friendship Race, reviews them all. Turn the sound on so the friends can read every instruction aloud. Each player’s stars are saved on this device.
