Adventure map

For grown-ups: Each game takes about 3 to 5 minutes and practices one skill: counting, shapes, patterns, comparing, adding and taking away, measuring, sorting, time, money, position words and number sense. The last game, the Friendship Race, reviews them all. The first three stops are open; finishing a stop opens the next one. Turn the sound on so the friends can read every instruction aloud. Each player’s stars are saved on this device.
