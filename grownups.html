About the games

For grown-ups: Each game takes about 3 to 5 minutes and practices one skill: counting, shapes, patterns, comparing, adding and taking away, measuring, sorting, time, money, position words and number sense. The last game, the Friendship Race, reviews them all. The first three stops are open; finishing a stop opens the next one. Turn the sound on so the friends can read every instruction aloud. Each player’s stars are saved on this device.

Stars: ★★★ for 90% or more right on the first or second try, ★★ for 70% or more, and ★ for finishing. A hint appears after two tries, so children are never stuck. Mastery uses the last three finished plays of a stop: below 70% shows Needs practice, 90% and up shows Mastered.

Privacy: Data stays on this device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. We only keep a first name or nickname, a picture choice, an optional age and the game results. Clearing this browser’s site data removes everything.
